Our Story

Loops in Threads was born from a simple love of yarn and the joy of creating something with your hands. Every bag, doll, top, and keychain is crocheted individually — no machines, no mass production.

We believe handmade is the most personal form of art. Each stitch carries intention, warmth, and care. When you hold one of our pieces, you're holding hours of love.
